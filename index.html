A friendly note on its purposeOur waterless cleaner solution is not a replacement for a pressure wash. It is designed for everyday upkeep: lifting light dust and grime, restoring shine between full washes, and cutting down the water you use. For heavy mud or deep-set dirt, a pressure wash remains the right method.
Recommended Advice

Please keep clean cloths ready. For best results, use clean, stain-free cloths, at least 3 pieces, for cleaning. Fresh cloths lift dirt safely instead of dragging it across your paint.

Do not directly spray on the vehicle surface. Spray on a clean and dry microfibre cloth for easy and better results, with cost saving on effective use. Use about 25 ml or less per clean.
